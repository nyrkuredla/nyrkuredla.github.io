Ontology

A formal system for defining the concepts within a domain and the relationships between them. Where a knowledge graph shows you the map, an ontology defines the rules for what kinds of things can appear on the map and how they're allowed to connect — the vocabulary and grammar of a knowledge system.

Discipline ensuring that people (and agents) have access to the information they need through systematic organization. There are many *many* sub-disciplines of KM work, but all are focused on ensuring that information is available, findable, relevant, and trustworthy.
