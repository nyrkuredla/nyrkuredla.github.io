Topic #0004

Knowledge Management

Field Note #DISCLOSURE-02
AI used for structuring, drafting, and other "initialization"; later editing and review by me. Some AI-generated language may remain in the final copy.
View Policy
Abstract

Discipline ensuring that people (and agents) have access to the information they need through systematic organization. There are many *many* sub-disciplines of KM work, but all are focused on ensuring that information is available, findable, relevant, and trustworthy.

Read More
What Is KM? Knowledge Management Explained , Michael E.D. Koenig, Ph.D., KMWorld

Deep-dive primer on Knowledge Management from KMWorld — its origins in management consulting, Davenport's classic definition, and the three-stage evolution from technology-first (best practices) to human factors (communities of practice) to findability (taxonomy and enterprise search).
